# Seasonal Agriculture Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes agricultural data to understand how agricultural performance varies across different seasons.

The analysis focuses on crop productivity, environmental conditions, resource utilization, economic performance, and agricultural risk. Data analysis and visualization techniques are used to identify seasonal patterns, differences, and relationships within the dataset.

## Objectives

- Explore and understand the agricultural dataset.
- Clean and prepare the data for analysis.
- Compare agricultural performance across different seasons.
- Analyze crop yield and production patterns.
- Study resource utilization such as water, fertilizer, and pesticides.
- Examine environmental conditions such as rainfall, temperature, humidity, and soil moisture.
- Compare revenue, cost, and profit across seasons.
- Analyze relationships between environmental, resource, and agricultural variables.
- Identify significant variations and unusual patterns.
- Generate insights and recommendations for seasonal agricultural planning.

## Dataset

The dataset contains **4,000 records and 28 attributes** related to agricultural activities.

## Key Analysis Areas

### Seasonal Performance
Comparison of agricultural productivity across Kharif, Rabi, and Zaid seasons using measures such as yield and production.

### Economic Performance
Analysis of revenue, cost, and profit to understand differences in economic outcomes across seasons.

### Resource Utilization
Analysis of water usage and water-use efficiency along with fertilizer and pesticide usage.

### Environmental Conditions
Comparison of rainfall, temperature, humidity, soil moisture, and other environmental conditions across seasons.

### Crop and Relationship Analysis
Analysis of crop-wise seasonal performance and relationships between environmental, resource, and agricultural variables.
